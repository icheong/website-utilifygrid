1. Organize & Manage (32 Tools)
These tools focus on the structure of the PDF file.

Merge PDF: Combine multiple files with drag-and-drop reordering.

Split PDF: Divide by page ranges or extract all pages.

Organize PDF: A visual workspace to reorder, duplicate, or delete pages.

Extract Pages: Save specific selections as a new file.

Delete Pages: Remove specific pages visually.

Rotate PDF: Change orientation of specific or all pages.

Reverse Pages: Instantly flip the page order.

OCR PDF: Convert scanned PDFs into searchable/selectable text.

Alternate Merge: Merge two PDFs by alternating their pages (useful for front/back scans).

N-Up PDF: Layout multiple pages onto a single sheet (e.g., 2-up or 4-up).

2. Edit & Annotate (26 Tools)
Content modification tools.

Edit PDF: Add text, images, shapes, and freehand drawings.

Sign PDF: Create signatures (draw, type, or upload) and place them on pages.

Annotate PDF: Highlight, underline, and add comments.

Add Watermark: Overlay text or images with transparency control.

Header and Footer: Add headers/footers with custom text to the top and/or bottom of every page including dynamic page numbering.

Bates Numbering: For legal indexing and identification.

Crop PDF: Trim margins or specific areas.

3. Convert to PDF (23 Tools)
Image to PDF: Supports JPG, PNG, TIFF, BMP, WEBP, GIF, and HEIC.

Office to PDF: Word (.docx), Excel (.xlsx), and PowerPoint (.pptx).

Text/Markdown to PDF: Convert raw text or formatted markdown.

HTML to PDF: Convert a URL or HTML file.

4. Convert from PDF (21 Tools)
PDF to Image: Export pages as JPG, PNG, or TIFF.

PDF to Office: Convert back to Word, Excel, or PowerPoint.

PDF to Text: Raw text extraction or OCR-based extraction.

PDF to HTML: Convert PDF structure into a webpage.

5. Optimize & Repair (16 Tools)
Compress PDF: Options for "Extreme," "Recommended," and "Low" compression.

Repair PDF: Fix corrupted or unreadable PDF files.

Flatten PDF: Merge all form fields and annotations into the background layer.

Metadata Editor: View and edit Author, Title, Keywords, and Subject.

6. Secure PDF (13 Tools)
Encrypt PDF: Set passwords and restrict permissions (printing, copying).

Decrypt PDF: Remove passwords from known protected files.

Redact PDF: Permanently black out sensitive information.

Sanitize PDF: Remove all hidden metadata and private information.
