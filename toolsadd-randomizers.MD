1. Coin Flipper
What it does: A simple binary choice generator (Heads or Tails).

The Utility: Perfect for quick, zero-effort decision-making or settling a simple bet. Use the animation of a coin spinning in the air and landing on either heads or tails.

2. Random Number Generator (RNG)
What it does: Allows users to input a minimum and maximum range (e.g., 1 to 100) and spits out a truly random integer within that boundary.

The Utility: A total staple for giveaways, statistical sampling, or choosing a winner from a numbered spreadsheet. Use the animation of a number rolling in the air and landing on the chosen number.

3. Team Picker / Group Maker
What it does: The user pastes a list of names and inputs how many teams or groups they need. The tool evenly distributes the names randomly.

The Utility: Incredibly popular for teachers setting up classroom projects, managers organizing workshops, or friends setting up video game matches.

4. Letter / Alphabet Randomizer
What it does: Spits out a random letter from A to Z, with toggles to exclude low-frequency letters (like Q, X, or Z).

The Utility: Great for classroom vocabulary games, or as a tool for playing games like Scattergories at home.

5. Yes / No Wheel (Decision Spinner)
What it does: A visual wheel that spins and lands on either "Yes", "No", or "Maybe".

The Utility: A more playful, interactive version of a coin flip for users dealing with decision fatigue.

6. Date / Time Randomizer
What it does: Users select a date range (e.g., Jan 1st to Dec 31st), and the tool selects a completely random day, month, and time.

The Utility: Used by writers looking for historical settings, developers generating dummy data, or couples trying to pick a random weekend for a trip.

7. Dice Roll Simulator
What it does: Users select how many dice to roll (1-6) and what type of dice (4-sided, 6-sided, 8-sided, 10-sided, 12-sided, 20-sided). The tool rolls the dice and displays the results.

The Utility: Popular for tabletop role-playing games, board games, or any situation where a user needs to simulate the rolling of dice. It can also show the animation of the dice rolling and landing on the chosen number.

8. Outfit Generator
What it does: Users select the weather conditions (hot, cold, rainy, snowy, etc.) and the tool suggests an outfit based on the weather. Use the animation of a person getting dressed in the suggested outfit with the weather in their background.

The Utility: Popular for people who are unsure of what to wear based on the weather.
